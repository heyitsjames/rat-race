Saddling up the rats…

Rat Race: the After Dark screensaver, playable in your browser

Rat Race was a screensaver module in After Dark 3.0, released by Berkeley Systems in 1994, with programming by Andrew Armstrong and art by Matt Small. Rats line up at the gates and race round an oval track to the William Tell Overture, but they are easily distracted: they stop to sniff, take naps and turn round to run the wrong way. The results board in the infield shows Win, Place and Show, and close races go to a photo finish.

This is a faithful browser reconstruction built from the original module's artwork, sounds and music.

How to play

  • Set the rats' Training, from Drop Out to Professional (plus Cracked), then press Run Race.
  • Choose how many rats race (3 to 8), turn on Competitive to keep every place contested, add Expanded names, or use Only expanded names.
  • When the race ends, Restart runs the same rats again with fresh luck; click the results board to see every rat's place and time.
  • Keys: Enter runs a race, Space pauses, R restarts, F goes fullscreen, M turns sound on or off, Esc returns to the menu.

About

A browser reconstruction of the After Dark module Rat Race 3.0, ©1994 Berkeley Systems, Inc. Programming by Andrew Armstrong. Art by Matt Small.

All artwork, sounds and music are the original resources, recovered from the module and the shared AD 3.0 Sound library. Timing, layout and behaviour are partly reconstructed; see docs/RECONSTRUCTION.md.
